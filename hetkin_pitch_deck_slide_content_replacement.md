# Replacement Section

## Slide content to build from (rewrite freely, keep facts and order intent)

The deck must be built around the following structure.

For **every slide**, Claude must define and implement:

-   Slide Objective
-   Visible Slide Copy
-   What the Presenter Says
-   Primary Visual
-   Design Intent
-   Layout Guidance
-   Motion Guidance
-   Transition to Next Slide
-   Non‑Negotiable Constraints
-   Completion Criteria

The visible slide should remain minimal.

The presenter notes contain the complete story.

The audience should never read paragraphs on the slide.

# Slide 01 --- Cover

## Objective

Introduce the core promise in under three seconds.

## Visible Slide Copy

**Every Hour Has a Price**

Same production.

Lower electricity bill.

## What the Presenter Says

"Factories and growers should not need to cut production to lower
electricity cost. They need to know which work can safely happen at a
cheaper hour."

## Primary Visual

Minimal full-screen typography with a subtle hourly timeline.

## Design Intent

Confidence and curiosity.

## Layout Guidance

Large editorial headline. Small supporting statement. Extensive
whitespace.

## Motion

Simple fade.

## Transition

Zoom into Hendrik's world.

## Constraints

Never mention software.

## Completion Criteria

Promise understood within three seconds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Slide 02 --- The Moment

## Objective

Introduce Hendrik and his daily reality.

## Visible Slide Copy

**8,000+ kWh a day.**

Some of it, at the worst possible hour.

## What the Presenter Says

Meet Hendrik. He runs production at a mid-sized greenhouse in Finland.
Lighting, irrigation and climate control run continuously. Last winter
electricity became one of his largest operating costs. Hendrik doesn't
have an electricity problem. He has a timing problem.

## Primary Visual

Minimal greenhouse process illustration with highlighted expensive
hours.

## Design Intent

Empathy.

## Constraints

Do not introduce Hetkin yet.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
